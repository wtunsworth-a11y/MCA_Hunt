# Setting up the MCA Hunting Survey app — for interviewers

This app lets you record interviews on your phone **with no internet**. You set
it up **once** (this needs internet or the file your supervisor sends you), and
after that it works anywhere — in the bush, in the village, with the phone in
airplane mode.

Please read this once before your first interview. It takes about 5 minutes to
set up.

---

## Before you start

- Use an **Android phone** with the **Chrome** browser (the default on most
  phones). This works best.
- Make sure your phone's **date and time are correct** (Settings → System →
  Date & time → "Automatic"). The app uses the time to record when interviews
  happen.
- You will be asked to allow **Location** — please tap **Allow**. The app only
  records where the *interview* takes place. **It never records where anyone
  hunts, fishes or gathers.**

---

## Setting it up

Your supervisor will send you the app in **one of two ways**. Follow the
matching steps.

### Method 1 — You received a LINK (recommended)

1. **While you have internet**, tap the link. The app opens in Chrome.
2. Tap the **⋮ menu** (three dots, top right) → **"Add to Home screen"** (some
   phones say **"Install app"**).
3. Confirm. You now have an **MCA Hunt** icon on your home screen, like any app.
4. Open it from that icon from now on. **You do not need internet again.**

### Method 2 — You received a FILE (`MCA_Hunt_Survey.html`)

1. Save the file to your phone (e.g. accept the download, or save the email
   attachment). Remember where it is — **do not move or rename it afterwards**,
   or the app will not find your saved interviews.
2. Open the file. If your phone asks which app to use, choose **Chrome**.
3. The app opens. (Optional: use Chrome's **⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen"** to
   make it easier to reopen.)

> **Which is better?** The **link** method is more reliable for a multi-day
> field trip. Use the file method only if you cannot get the link.

---

## First time you open it

1. It asks for your **interviewer name or ID** (for example your name, or
   `INT-03`). Type it and tap **Save and continue**. This is stored on your
   phone and attached to every interview you do, so we know who collected what.
2. You will land on the **home screen**, showing your list of interviews (empty
   at first).

**Test it now:** turn on **airplane mode**, then do steps below with a pretend
respondent to check everything works offline. You can delete the test interview
afterwards from the home screen.

---

## Doing an interview

1. On the home screen tap **➕ New interview**.
2. **Read the consent script aloud.** If the person agrees, tap **"Consent
   given — start"**. If they do not agree, tap **"Consent declined — cancel"** —
   nothing is saved.
3. The app fills in Module A automatically:
   - It gets the **GPS** for where you are sitting (you may see "Getting GPS…"
     then "GPS captured"). If it says **"GPS missing"**, that's OK — tap **Retry
     GPS** if you have a moment, otherwise just carry on. The interview is not
     blocked.
   - Once you pick the **Zone**, a **Respondent ID** appears automatically
     (e.g. `Z01-ABC-001`). You never type this yourself.
4. Work through the modules **A → I** using the **Next** button. Use **Back** or
   the round **A B C … I** buttons at the top to go back and change anything.
5. **Every question can be left blank.** If someone doesn't want to answer, or
   doesn't know, just leave it and move on — the app will never stop you.
6. At the end you reach a **Review** screen showing what was entered. Check it,
   then tap **Complete interview**.

**Your progress saves automatically after every tap.** If your battery dies or
you close the app by accident mid-interview, just reopen it and tap the
interview in your list to carry on where you left off.

---

## Tips for the field

- You can do **many interviews on one phone** across several days before sending
  anything in. They all stay in your list.
- The two big tick-grids (methods, and months) **scroll sideways** — swipe left
  and right to see all the columns. The first column (the animal) stays put.
- In Module F you record two trips — **most recent** and **most successful in
  the last 12 months**. They are clearly labelled; fill in both.
- Some questions only show up when relevant (for example the "other, specify"
  box only appears if you chose "other"). That's normal.

---

## Sending your interviews to the supervisor

When you next have internet or can transfer files:

1. Open the app → home screen.
2. Tap **Export CSV (with names)** (or **without names** if your supervisor
   asked for anonymised data).
3. Your phone saves a `.csv` file and/or offers to **share** it. Send it to your
   supervisor by **email, WhatsApp, or file transfer** — whatever works.
4. That's it. Your interviews stay on your phone too (they're marked
   "exported"), so nothing is lost.

---

## If something goes wrong

- **App looks empty / lists gone:** make sure you opened it the **same way** as
  before (same icon, or same file in the same place). If you used the file
  method and moved the file, put it back where it was.
- **GPS never works:** carry on — it's optional and the interview is still
  valid, just flagged as "no GPS".
- **Not sure a change saved:** it did — the app saves after every tap. When in
  doubt, go back a module and check.
- **Anything else:** note it down and tell your supervisor. Do **not** delete
  interviews unless you're sure.
