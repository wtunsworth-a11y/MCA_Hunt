# paper/

Working files for the MCA hunting & fishing results paper (full scientific
manuscript, targeting a specialist conservation journal).

## Contents

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `manuscript.md` | IMRaD scaffold. Methods drafted in full; Introduction skeleton with citation-status flags. Fishing treated as a stated gap, not padded. |
| `refs_verified.md` | annotated reference list. **[VERIFIED]** = confirmed from the file's own front matter and/or a corroborating source; **[confirm]** = a detail still to pin down. PDFs are in the Drive "Papers" folder. |
| `discussion_notes.md` | field-context notes to carry into the write-up: the uneven natural baseline (between-zone differences reflect habitat/elevation, not a hunting effect); Zone 6 elevation (montane fauna availability, not depletion); echidna voucher; codebook reminders. |

## Framing (see ../CLAUDE.md)

Report; don't sell. No booster or confirmation-bias language. The four T's
(Target / Tool / Tenure / Timing) is an **external comparative framework**
(Pattiselanno et al. 2024), named as a researcher's lens — not an indigenous
system. Hunting/fishing are legitimate landowner resource use; species and
takes are neutral records.

## Data

Results are computed from the v2.5.0 main survey (see `../analysis/`). **No
survey data lives in this repo** — it stays in the Google Drive Survey Data
folder. Regenerate figures locally from a downloaded consolidated CSV.
